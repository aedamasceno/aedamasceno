Current initiative: Project AEGIS
Project AEGIS is a proposed free and open-source initiative exploring privacy-first AI assistance for law enforcement and public safety.

Planned areas of research and development:
- Incident documentation: Assist with organizing officer-provided facts into clear narratives, without generating or inventing evidence.
- Reference retrieval: Support citation-grounded searches of authorized laws, policies, and procedures, with human verification.
- Training: Provide scenario-based practice for communication, de-escalation, and crisis response.
- Local AI deployment: Evaluate offline and on-premises inference to reduce reliance on external services.
- Accessible hardware: Benchmark higher-end development systems and adapt working solutions for smaller agency budgets.
Status: Early planning and prototyping. No operational deployment or agency endorsement is claimed.
Project principles
- Free and open source, with published setup instructions and reproducible evaluations.
- Privacy and security by design, using synthetic or appropriately authorized data for development.
- Human oversight for all real-world decisions; never autonomous arrest, detention, use-of-force, identification, or threat-scoring decisions.
- Transparent reporting of model limitations, hallucination risks, and evaluation results.
Technical focus
- AI: Local LLMs, AI agents, inference benchmarking, retrieval-augmented generation
- Infrastructure: Linux, containers, networking, storage, observability, self-hosting
- Networking & collaboration: Cisco Collaboration, SIP, voice infrastructure, enterprise network design
- Open source: Linux-native applications and documentation-driven, reproducible deployments
